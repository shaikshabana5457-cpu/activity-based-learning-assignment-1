UTSAV is a college festival that brings together students from different departments to showcase their skills, exchange ideas, learn new technologies, and celebrate culture and creativity.

Participate in exciting competitions, interactive workshops, cultural performances, and fun activities!

Festival Rules and Guidelines

  1. All participants must carry their college ID cards.
  2. Register for events using the registration form.
  3. Participants must arrive at the venue before the event begins.
  4. Follow the instructions given by event coordinators.
  5. Maintain discipline and respect all participants.
  6. Plagiarism and unfair practices are not allowed in competitions.
  7. Follow the safety rules during workshops and technical events.
  8. The organizers' final decisions will apply to event results.
